Project Brief: Custom Website Development for Anderson Futbol Lab

Overview:
We are developing a new high-performance, responsive website for "Anderson Futbol Lab", a premier soccer development program based in Mississauga, Ontario. The site will be built and hosted on Replit using modern web standards (e.g., React, Next.js, HTML/CSS, Tailwind CSS).

Design Benchmark & Vibe:
* Layout & Structure Model: https://www.superiorstriker.com/
  - We want to replicate the high-energy, sleek, and high-converting flow of Superior Striker (hero video/image sections, bold typography, elite athletic branding, clear registration funnels).
* Existing Brand Reference: https://www.andersonfutbollab.com/

Brand Identity & Color Palette (Extracted from Logo):
Maintain strictly the existing brand color palette derived from the official logo crest:
* Primary Navy Blue: Core headers, deep section backgrounds, primary text accent.
* Athletic Gold: CTA buttons, crowns/highlights, secondary badges, active states.
* Crimson Red: Accent lines, borders, alert badges, micro-interactions.
* Clean White / Light Gray: Background contrast and primary readability text.

Core Objectives & Registration Funnel:
The primary focus of the site is lead generation and user intake. We need intuitive forms and clear calls-to-action (CTAs) allowing users to register or inquire about:
1. Trial Dates
2. Junior Elite Program
3. General Enquiries / Other Programs

Key Pages & Features:
1. Home Page: Dynamic hero section, program teaser cards, high-impact imagery, testimonials/social proof, and persistent CTA buttons.
2. About Us: Philosophy, elite coaching staff, and club mission statement.
3. Programs: Detailed breakdown of offerings, with heavy emphasis on the "Junior Elite Program".
4. Trial Registration: Dedicated intake form with dropdowns asking users what program/dates they are registering for.
5. Contact Page & Footer: Direct access to contact details and embedded Instagram feed/links.

Technical Requirements (Replit Build):
* Mobile-First Responsive Design (Optimized for Instagram ad/bio link traffic).
* Clean, modular codebase suitable for Replit deployment.
* Lightweight assets to ensure maximum page speed and fast loading times.
* Integrated form handling (e.g., Web3Forms, Formspree, or backend endpoint) to send lead notifications directly to our email addresses.
